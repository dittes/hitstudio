Privacy

HitStudio runs entirely in your browser. Projects, songs, recordings and settings are stored only on your device (browser storage). No audio is uploaded, there are no user accounts, no tracking and no advertising cookies.

If you play a radio station from the library, your browser connects directly to that station's server, so the station receives your IP address as with any audio stream. When you load this site, the hosting provider processes technically necessary server log data (for example IP address, time and requested file).

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Berlin this is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.
