# HitStudio — Product Hunt launch film: storylines

**The one thing:** a full music studio that runs privately in your browser tab, from an idea to a finished, visualized song.
**Spine:** one song, generated from the seed `lofi-chill-7`, travels through the whole film.
**Facts used (README):** Create / Edit / Mix / EQ / DJ / Jam / Sampler / Tools / Visualize; 12 genres; shareable seed codes; 4×4 sampler, 243 sounds; 18 visual scenes; MP4 export at 1080p; no account, no upload, no backend.

Assumptions: 30–40 s, 1920×1080 @ 30 fps, no voiceover, music made in HitStudio itself (songsmith + sampler kit, no paid items). Screens captured from the local app.

---

## A. One seed, one song  (recommended — a stranger follows one song being made)
- **Device / form:** one object across the app · object-to-object chain.
- A friend's message holds a code, `lofi-chill-7`. Clicked, it becomes a song that grows section by section — beat, melody, chords, pads, mix — until it bursts into a full-screen visual.
- **First 2 s:** a chat bubble "try this one 🔥 lofi-chill-7"; the code lifts out of the bubble and drops into HitStudio's Generate tab, and the drum grid lights up on the downbeat.
- **Beats:** hook (code arrives) → tension (blank DAW, a silent grid) → reveal "HitStudio" → proof: generate · finger-drum the pads · pull a fader · crossfade on DJ → payoff: Visualize scene fills the frame → claim "A whole studio. One browser tab." → end card hitstudio.org.
- **Look:** HitStudio's own dark theme and gradient accents; real UI at 1.6–2×; the gradient used only on the reveal and end card.
- **Sound:** the song itself is the score; pad hits and fader moves land on visible actions.

## B. Rewind the hit
- **Device / form:** the result first · an endless zoom in reverse.
- A full-screen visualizer pulses; the camera pulls back through the player into the studio, then rewinds through how the track was made — mix, melody, a single sampler pad.
- **First 2 s:** the Cymatic bloom scene at full bleed, mid-drop.
- **Beats:** payoff first → pull back into the app → rewind: mix → chords → beat → one pad → snap forward to the drop → claim → end card.
- **Look:** saturated visualizer worlds alternating with the calm studio UI.
- **Sound:** the drop, a tape-rewind gesture, then the track rebuilding stem by stem.

## C. Sixteen pads  (the bold one)
- **Device / form:** type carries the argument · cut on the beat.
- The 4×4 sampler is the frame. Each pad hit cuts to a new section of the studio or a giant word: "No install." "No account." "No upload."
- **First 2 s:** a cursor slams pad 1 (kick) — the frame cuts to the beat grid on the same frame.
- **Beats:** pad hits drive cuts → words land on the snare → the grid rebuilds into the DJ decks → visual scene → end card built from the pads lighting up the logo.
- **Look:** flat colour fields from the brand gradient, huge type, real pads.
- **Sound:** the factory kit played live; every cut is a hit.
